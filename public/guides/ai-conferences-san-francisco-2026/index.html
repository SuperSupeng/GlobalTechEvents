If a friend asked which San Francisco AI conference is still worth booking in 2026, I would not send them to Moscone. As of 2026-10-16 the only one still ahead inside the city is Reverie, one day on November 5 at the Contemporary Jewish Museum, and I would book it only if the work is training data or multimodal models. The multi-day builder weeks I would still book are outside the city: PyTorch Conference and AGNTCon in San Jose, then ODSC in Burlingame.

What I count as San Francisco

I put a row in the San Francisco table only when the organizer prints a date and a venue inside the city. Moscone, Fort Mason, Pier 48, and the Contemporary Jewish Museum count. The airport hotel in Burlingame, and the convention center in San Jose, go in the Bay Area section. I will not call Burlingame or San Jose a San Francisco trip because a homepage used that city name.

The program is AI-primary, or it is a major tech event whose official agenda is built around AI. I order each table by date. If the organizer site had no date when I read it on 2026-10-16, I left the event off. I am not filling a blank date.

As of 2026-10-16, one AI conference is still ahead at a San Francisco venue. Reverie is a single day. LanceDB is the host, and the agenda is technical talks and breakouts for researchers and engineers. If you need a multi-day builder program that is still bookable, I would look outside the city, in the next section. I would not pretend Moscone still has an AI week left this year.

Who I would send

I pick from the role that matches the trip. I fill a cell only when the organizer site states the audience or the format. A thin agenda stays at Watch.

AI and platform engineers

Still ahead, I would book Reverie on November 5 if the work is training data or multimodal models, and that is the only day I would still book inside the city. I would book PyTorch Conference on October 20 to 21 if the work is that open-source stack, and AGNTCon + MCPCon on October 22 to 23 if the work is agents or MCP. Both of those are in San Jose. I would book ODSC AI West on October 27 to 29 only if Burlingame is an acceptable venue for training days. Already held, and worth a next-cycle look only on a matching stack: AI Engineer World's Fair for applied engineering workshops, Microsoft Build for the Microsoft agent and Azure stack, OpenAI DevDay for the OpenAI API, Databricks if the platform is Databricks. Build and DevDay both used Fort Mason. I would not stack them. They are different APIs.

Founders and go-to-market

Reverie is a skip when the goal is buyer density. LanceDB is hosting a technical day for researchers and engineers, not a fundraising floor. TechCrunch Disrupt, October 13 to 15 at Moscone West, was the startup and investor week, and it has ended. Dreamforce was the Salesforce buyer week, and it has ended. I would not use one as a substitute for the other. Tech Week SF, October 5 to 11, was a week of separately hosted events, and it has ended. I am not scoring that week.

Enterprise buyers

Dreamforce is the Salesforce week. Sessions from the 2026 edition are on Salesforce+, and the organizer lists September 21 to 23, 2027 in San Francisco. HumanX, April 6 to 9 at Moscone South, is the leadership week that has already run. I would not send a Databricks team there for lakehouse training. Databricks is the lakehouse week. ODSC lists an AI X Leadership Summit on October 27 to 28 inside the Burlingame event. That is the buyer slice still ahead, and it is not a Moscone buyer hall. I would book the estate you are actually buying.

Researchers

Reverie is the technical day still ahead in the city. It is a LanceDB summit on training data, world models, and multimodal systems. NeurIPS, ICML, and CVPR are the paper venues. I am not assigning them a 2026 city here, and I am not ranking them against Reverie. AI Engineer World's Fair and The AI Conference were in-person San Francisco programs with research-adjacent talks, and both have ended. The AI Conference FAQ says that event is in person only. A paper venue is a different job, and I am not using this page to pick one.

FAQ

Which San Francisco AI conferences are still upcoming in 2026?

As of 2026-10-16, I would still book Reverie on November 5 at the Contemporary Jewish Museum, and that is the only AI conference still ahead inside San Francisco. Is San Jose or the SFO airport hotel the same as San Francisco for this guide?

No. When I say San Francisco I mean a venue in the city, such as Moscone, Fort Mason, Pier 48, or the Contemporary Jewish Museum.
