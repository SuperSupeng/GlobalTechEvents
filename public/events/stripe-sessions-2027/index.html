Stripe Sessions 2027: Dates, Location & Event Guide

Stripe Sessions is the Stripe conference for payments, billing, and internet economy teams. Sessions 2027 is 4-6 May 2027 at Moscone North and South in San Francisco. Go if you run payments, billing, or agent commerce on Stripe. Skip it if you only need a model-provider developer day. Passes and the call for proposals are on the official site.

FAQ

When is Stripe Sessions 2027?

Stripe Sessions 2027 is 4-6 May 2027. The official site lists Tuesday 4 May, Wednesday 5 May, and Thursday 6 May.

Where is Stripe Sessions 2027?

It is at Moscone North and South in San Francisco. The official page checked on 16 October 2026 does not describe a virtual pass.

What do passes and talk proposals cost?

The official site shows $599 and $999 under early-bird admission, and says early-bird rates expire on 27 January 2027. Talk proposals close on 3 November 2026. There is no fee to present.
