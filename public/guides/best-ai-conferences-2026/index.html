Best by role

Most people should pick one flagship, one regional, and optionally one specialty—not every row.

Builders, AI engineers, and agent teams

Start with OpenAI DevDay and Microsoft Build for builder/API/agent narrative, GTC for infra and research-to-product depth, Databricks Data + AI Summit for practitioners, and SuperAI for Asia industry networking.

Enterprise buyers and IT leaders

Prefer AWS re:Invent and Google Cloud Next for hyperscaler roadmaps, Dreamforce for enterprise SaaS/Agentforce narratives, and GITEX GLOBAL for MENA enterprise and government buyers.

Founders, operators, and press

CES for consumer and hardware AI launches, VivaTech and Web Summit for Europe startup attention, SuperAI for Asia founder density.

Infra, hardware, and chip ecosystem

GTC is the flagship. CES is the consumer/hardware stage. COMPUTEX and similar hardware shows sit outside this main table—see the AI-tracks section and the calendar.

Best by region

Geography often decides more than brand preference. Cluster trips when cities overlap.

North America

CES and re:Invent / Cloud Next lean Las Vegas. GTC is San Jose. Build, DevDay, Data + AI Summit, and Dreamforce cluster around San Francisco / Bay Area.

Europe

VivaTech (Paris) and Web Summit (Lisbon) are the two Europe rows in this shortlist.

Middle East and Asia

GITEX GLOBAL anchors Dubai / MENA enterprise. SuperAI anchors Singapore / Asia AI industry.

FAQ

What are the best AI conferences in 2026?

For industry (not academic) AI, start with NVIDIA GTC, OpenAI DevDay, AWS re:Invent, Google Cloud Next, Microsoft Build, Databricks Data + AI Summit, SuperAI, CES, GITEX GLOBAL, and VivaTech. Pick by role and region. As of 2026-10-14, several editions have already run; Web Summit, re:Invent, and GITEX remain upcoming.

Are NeurIPS, ICML, or CVPR on this list?

No. Those are research venues with CFP and peer-review rhythms. This guide covers industry, product, cloud, and startup AI events that help you decide whether to attend or track for work.

Which AI conference is best for developers versus enterprise buyers?

Developers and agent teams usually get more from DevDay, Build, GTC, Data + AI Summit, and SuperAI. Enterprise buyers and IT leaders usually get more from re:Invent, Cloud Next, Dreamforce, and GITEX. Founders and press often prefer CES, VivaTech, and Web Summit.
