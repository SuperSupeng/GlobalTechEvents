FAQ

When and where is NeurIPS 2026?

As of 16 October 2026, the official site lists the main meeting in Sydney on 6-12 December 2026. Expo day is 6 December, tutorials are 7 December, the main conference is 8-10 December, and workshops are 11-12 December.

Are there other NeurIPS 2026 cities?

Yes. Official satellite meetings are in Atlanta and Paris on 9-13 December 2026. This page uses Sydney as the main meeting. Confirm which city you mean on neurips.cc.

Should I go?

Go if you are presenting, reading the research program, or recruiting researchers. Skip it if you want an industry buyer expo.
