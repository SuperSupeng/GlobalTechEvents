How we chose

A row sits in the San Francisco table when the organizer prints a date and a venue inside the city. Moscone, Fort Mason, Pier 48, and the Contemporary Jewish Museum qualify. The airport hotel in Burlingame, and the convention center in San Jose, are in the Bay Area section.

The program is AI-primary, or it is a major tech event whose official agenda is built around AI. Order inside each table is by date. Criteria are the venue, the program, and an official URL.

Every date below was read from the organizer site on 2026-10-16. An event with no date on that site is left off the tables.

Still ahead: Reverie on November 5 if the work is training data or multimodal models; PyTorch Conference on October 20 to 21 if the work is that open-source stack; AGNTCon + MCPCon on October 22 to 23 if the work is agents or MCP; ODSC AI West on October 27 to 29 if Burlingame is an acceptable venue for training days. Already held, and worth a next-cycle look only on a matching stack: AI Engineer World's Fair for applied engineering, Microsoft Build for the Microsoft platform, OpenAI DevDay for the OpenAI API, Databricks if the platform is Databricks.

Founders and go-to-market

Reverie is a skip when the goal is buyer density. The organizer designs that day for researchers and engineers. Tech Week SF, October 5 to 11, was a week of separately hosted events, and it has ended. This page does not score that week.

Enterprise buyers

Dreamforce is the Salesforce week. Sessions from the 2026 edition are on Salesforce+, and the organizer lists September 21 to 23, 2027 in San Francisco. HumanX, April 6 to 9 at Moscone South, is the leadership week that has already run. Databricks is the lakehouse week. ODSC lists an AI X Leadership Summit on October 27 to 28 inside the Burlingame event. Book the estate you are actually buying.

Researchers

Reverie is the technical day still ahead in the city. It is a LanceDB summit on training data, not a peer-reviewed conference. NeurIPS, ICML, and CVPR are the paper venues. This page does not assign them a 2026 city. AI Engineer World's Fair and The AI Conference were in-person San Francisco programs with research-adjacent talks, and both have ended. The AI Conference FAQ says that event is in person only.

Which San Francisco AI conferences are still upcoming in 2026?
