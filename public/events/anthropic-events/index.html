FAQ

Which Code with Claude dates did Anthropic print for 2026?

San Francisco on 6 May 2026, London on 19 May 2026, and Tokyo on 10 June 2026. Each city also has an Extended day on the next date: 7 May, 20 May, and 11 June 2026.

Is there one Code with Claude venue for 2026?

No. The 2026 city pages name the city and do not name a hall. A 2025 news post at https://www.anthropic.com/news/Introducing-code-with-claude names The Midway for 22 May 2025. That address is not printed on the 2026 city pages.

Does the linked Code with Claude material list a 2027 date?

No. The Code with Claude pages linked on this guide do not list a 2027 date.
