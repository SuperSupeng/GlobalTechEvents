How to pick one to three

Leave with one flagship and one regional trip. Add a third event only when you already have named meetings there.

Builders

If you call the OpenAI API, the one-day Fort Mason briefing is the room, and it already ran, so watch the recap unless you are planning next cycle. If you ship Copilot or Azure, the two-day San Francisco builder briefing is the match. If the product is GPUs or physical AI, the San Jose week is the match.

Enterprise buyers

Book the five-day Las Vegas week only when AWS is the estate. Book the December Dubai floor when the buyer is in the Gulf. The Salesforce week and the Google Cloud week already ran. Repeat one of them next cycle only if that estate is yours.

Founders and press

Lisbon in early November is the upcoming founder-and-press week. Paris and the January Las Vegas consumer floor already ran. Pick the city where your next customers will stand.

When a stream is enough

Watch a recap when the value is the keynote. Fly when the value is a floor or a meeting you can name. That is the Dubai expo, the Lisbon halls, and any partner meeting already on your calendar. When a date moves, check the event calendar before you book flights.

Flagship snapshots

Use a snapshot when the table cell is too short to decide the trip.

NVIDIA GTC

The March San Jose week is the one place on this list where the agenda is chips, clusters, and machines that use them. Go next cycle if a GPU supply plan, an AI factory, or a robotics stack is why you travel. Skip it when you wanted an application API keynote. The two-day Copilot briefing and the Fort Mason day will not tell you the accelerator roadmap, and this week will not replace them.

OpenAI DevDay

The 2026 edition was one day at Fort Mason, not a week on a convention campus. Go next cycle only if your product calls the OpenAI API and you want that room. If you are comparing models you do not ship, watch the recap. A single day does not pay for a vague tour of San Francisco, and it is the wrong week when your platform is Azure, AWS, or a data lake.

AWS re:Invent

This is the trip still on the calendar. Five days in Las Vegas, Nov 30 to Dec 4, exist to hear what AWS will sell next year and to meet the partners who implement it. Go if AWS is where the workload runs. Skip if it does not. Lisbon will not brief you on that roadmap, and this week will not give you Lisbon-style founder density.

Microsoft Build

The 2026 edition was Jun 2 to 3 in San Francisco, a short developer briefing for Copilot, agent tooling, and Azure. Go next cycle if that is the stack you ship. Skip if you came for GPUs or for a startup expo. Two days is enough for the API story and a poor use of a long-haul flight for anyone outside that stack.

SuperAI

The 2026 edition was Jun 10 to 11 at Marina Bay Sands. The reason to go next cycle is the Asia industry room, not a hyperscaler keynote. Choose it over a US campus week when the customers, hires, or investors you need are in Singapore. Skip it as a stand-in for the San Jose chip week or the five-day AWS week. Those answer different questions on different continents.

CES

The January Las Vegas floor already ran. Next cycle it is still the consumer and hardware launch stage, which is why press and product teams go and why an infra team should stay home. Watch the recap when you only need the headlines.

Google Cloud Next

The April Las Vegas edition already ran. Next cycle, go for Gemini and Google Cloud purchasing decisions. If the estate is AWS or Azure, skip it instead of adding a third cloud week.

Databricks Data + AI Summit

The mid-June Moscone week already ran. Next cycle it is for teams whose job is the lakehouse and the models on top of it. It is the wrong room for a model-lab audience and the wrong room for a consumer launch floor.

VivaTech

Paris already ran. Professional days were Jun 17 to 19 and the public festival day was Jun 20. Next cycle, go for European founder and press density. Skip it when you need a closed vendor API briefing.

Dreamforce

Mid-September in San Francisco already ran. Next cycle, go only when Agentforce and the Salesforce estate are the purchase. GPU builders and API builders are in the wrong room.

Web Summit

Still upcoming, Nov 9 to 12 in Lisbon. Go for founder and press density, and treat AI as a track inside a general tech expo. Skip it when the trip has to produce one vendor's roadmap.

GITEX GLOBAL

Still upcoming, Dec 7 to 11 in Dubai. Go when Gulf government and enterprise buyers are the meetings. Skip when the buyers are Bay Area engineering teams. A stream is a weak substitute because the point is the floor.

Academic weeks are a different job

NeurIPS, ICML, and CVPR are not in either table. They run on papers and review deadlines. Use them when the work product is a paper. Use this page when the work product is a product decision, a partnership, or a purchase.

Events with an AI track that are not this shortlist

MWC, COMPUTEX, and similar hardware weeks carry AI sessions and still do not belong in these two tables. Smaller builder meetings such as AI Engineer also stay off this page. If the decision is one of the twelve brands above, stay here. If it is a hardware week, use the events directory.

FAQ

How do I pick one to three instead of the whole list?

Start from the stack you ship and the city your buyers visit. Book at most one upcoming event unless you already have named meetings at a second. Use the held table only to mark next year's repeat, not to plan this quarter.

When is watching remote enough?

Watch when the value is a keynote or an API announcement. Fly when the value is a floor or a meeting you can name. A recap covers the one-day Fort Mason API briefing. It does not cover a Dubai expo or a Lisbon founder week.

Should I choose an academic conference or an industry one?

Choose NeurIPS, ICML, or CVPR when you are submitting or hearing papers. Choose a row on this page when you are deciding a product, a stack, or a partnership. They solve different jobs.

Which 2026 AI conferences can I still book?

As of 2026-10-15, Web Summit, AWS re:Invent, and GITEX GLOBAL have not run. The other nine editions on this page already happened.

What if my stack is not AWS, Google, or Microsoft?

Then the five-day Las Vegas AWS week, the April Google Cloud week, and the two-day Copilot briefing are skips. Look at the March San Jose week for hardware and infra, Fort Mason if you call the OpenAI API, Marina Bay or Lisbon for a regional room, and Dubai if the buyer is in the Gulf.

What if a date here is wrong?

This page uses organizer dates and leaves unknowns blank rather than filling them in. The as-of date is 2026-10-15. Check the organizer site before you book. The Paris week is Jun 17 to 20, with professional days Jun 17 to 19 and a public day on Jun 20.
